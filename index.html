How SaveMEDICS Works

A clean, transparent, and direct loop designed to eliminate fraud and maximize clinical outcomes.

01

Mission Submission

Patients or family members submit a mission request. They upload government IDs, medical diagnoses, and official hospital estimate bills.

02

Triage & Verification

Our medical consensus board matches diagnosis data with hospital nodes, establishing patient identity and real clinical urgency.

03

Direct Clearing

Community support goes directly to a secure escrow dedicated to the hospital. On discharge, bills are paid directly to the hospital.
