Terms of Support

Effective Date: 28 May 2026

1. Purpose of Support

Save Medics exists to provide transparent, verified humanitarian support for individuals facing medical emergencies. Support may include financial aid, awareness, or connection to donors and organizations.

2. Nature of Support

  • All support is voluntary and based on donor willingness
  • Save Medics does not guarantee funding for any case
  • Support is not a loan and does not require repayment
  • Assistance may be partial, full, or not provided depending on availability of donors

3. Verification Requirement

Before receiving support:

  • All cases may undergo identity and medical verification
  • Supporting documents must be genuine and valid
  • Any false or misleading information will lead to rejection or removal

4. No Guarantee Clause

Save Medics does NOT guarantee:

  • That any listed case will receive donations
  • The amount of support a case may receive
  • The time taken to receive support
  • Continuous or recurring support

5. Transparency in Support

To maintain trust:

  • Public cases may show anonymized or partial details
  • Donation progress may be displayed publicly (if enabled)
  • Donor identity will remain private unless voluntarily shared

6. Misuse Prevention

Any attempt to misuse support includes:

  • Fake medical cases
  • Fraudulent documents
  • Misleading emotional appeals
  • Multiple submissions for the same fake case

Such actions will lead to permanent restriction from the platform.

7. Role of Save Medics

Save Medics acts only as a connecting platform between:

  • Patients in need
  • Verified donors
  • Support organizations

We do NOT act as a financial institution or medical provider.

8. Withdrawal or Update of Support

Support for any case may be paused, updated, or removed if new verification issues or policy violations are found.

9. User Responsibility

Users requesting support must:

  • Provide accurate and truthful information
  • Update case status when required
  • Use support only for stated medical purpose

10. Limitation of Liability

Save Medics is not responsible for:

  • Donation delays or failures
  • Misuse of funds after support is received
  • Third-party donor actions
  • Medical outcomes or treatment results

11. Changes to Terms

These Terms of Support may be updated anytime to improve transparency and platform safety.
