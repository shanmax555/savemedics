Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 28 May 2026

1. Overview

Save Medics is a humanitarian support platform designed to connect people in need with verified assistance through transparent, trust-based systems. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting any information shared with us.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information:

  • Basic identity details (name, contact number, email)
  • Patient or request-related information (medical need, hospital details, documents)
  • Verification documents uploaded by users
  • Device and usage data (browser type, IP address, analytics data)

3. How We Use Information

We use collected data to:

  • Verify patient or request authenticity
  • Prevent fraud and misuse of the platform
  • Connect verified cases with donors or support systems
  • Improve platform performance and user experience
  • Display aggregated, non-personal statistics (like number of cases supported)

4. Data Sharing

We do NOT sell user data.

We may share limited information only when necessary:

  • With verification team for authenticity checks
  • With donors (only anonymized or minimal required details)
  • With legal authorities if required by law

5. Data Storage & Security

We use secure databases and modern security practices to protect user data. However, no system is 100% secure, so users share information at their own discretion.

6. User Responsibility

Users must:

  • Provide only accurate and truthful information
  • Not upload fake medical requests or documents
  • Not misuse the platform for scams or fraud

7. Cookies

We may use cookies to improve user experience, analytics, and platform performance.

8. Third-Party Services

We may use services like hosting, analytics, or storage providers. These services have their own privacy policies.

9. Changes to Policy

We may update this policy anytime. Continued use of the platform means acceptance of updates.

10. Contact

For any privacy concerns, contact us at: savemedics@gmail.com
